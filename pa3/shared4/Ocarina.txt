Nintendo has kept the Ocarina of Time remake incredibly close to its chest for the past several years, only formally revealing the game this summer and holding gameplay footage until less than two months before its launch. Still, finally seeing a truly modernized version of one of the highest-regarded games ever is incredibly exciting. After getting a look at the reveal footage, it’s a good thing Ocarina of Time drops so soon – I’m not sure I’d be able to wait much longer.
